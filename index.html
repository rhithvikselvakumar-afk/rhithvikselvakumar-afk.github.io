Selected Projects

CORNELL / ENGRI 1100 · TEAM LAB

Nitrogen Laser — Pulse Amplifier Group

In my ENGRI lab at Cornell, I participated in a team project to build a nitrogen laser. I worked in the Pulse Amplifier group.

My role: Pulse Amplifier group member.

PROJECT 01 / Aerospace · CAD · Concept design

NASA HAS: Space Suit Concept

As part of the NASA HAS Moonshot experience, I helped develop a lunar and Martian space suit proposal. My work included 3D models of gloves, a helmet, and an arm-mounted HUD, alongside research into temperature regulation and glove functionality.

Contribution / outcome: Team project; my focus was component design and 3D modeling.

PROJECT 02 / Sustainable design · 3D modeling · Team leadership

Future City: Sustainable Ocean City

I led the 3D modeling component of a team project exploring a sustainable ocean city. Our design considered renewable energy, resource management, and the structural challenges of a marine environment.

Contribution / outcome: Our team received People’s Choice and Best Essay awards at the Future City international competition.

PROJECT 03 / CAD · 3D printing · Practical problem solving

Custom Parts & Classroom Tools

Through my school CAD club and work at Leap4ward, I designed and printed practical items including replacement parts, device holders, custom nameplates, and molecular model kits.

Contribution / outcome: I worked with teachers to understand their needs and develop designs for classroom use.

PROJECT 04 / Spatial design · CAD · Collaboration

Leap4ward: Center Layout Model

I developed a 3D model of a new learning center to help teachers explore classroom layouts before construction.

Contribution / outcome: The model supported discussions about how the learning spaces could be organized.

PROJECT 05 / Rocketry · Design · Flight

SNARKY: Model Rocket

Through the Rocket Science Club, I designed and launched a model rocket named SNARKY. I also helped teach students about rocketry and flight paths.

Contribution / outcome: The rocket reached an altitude of over 400 meters, as recorded in my résumé.
